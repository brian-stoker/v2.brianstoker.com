### selfactor

- the deploy's postflight check glimpsed a daemon that was still coming up and declared the deploy failed, for the second time that week
  - the reading is taken again inside the existing timeout and only a process still on the wrong version fails, and the case went in as a governed task and landed
  - the installed version had been running the whole time, so the fault was in the check that judged it
- the approval judge had no model route that answered, so no plan on the machine could be promoted to a task
  - the routes were checked one at a time and named separately in the report rather than the judge being blamed as a whole, and approval was back the same morning once a login was renewed

### hal

- realtime voice failed to start because the on-device English speech-recognition assets had never been downloaded; installing them through the local speech probe made the session start
- the number-pad zero key became a right arrow through the system key remapping, applied to every attached keyboard and checked afterwards
- the two .plan entries still sitting on disk as drafts went live, illustrated by the local generator while the hosted image models refused
