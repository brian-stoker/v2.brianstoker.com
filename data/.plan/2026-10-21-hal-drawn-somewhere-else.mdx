### hal

- replaced a wait for an idle machine with a hosted image call
  - `scripts/grok_image.py` requests one 1280x720 frame and returns in about fifteen seconds, off this host, so a post gets its picture without warming a local generator
  - the hero step tries that route first and keeps the idle-gated local fallback for when the call fails
- rewrote the daily plan rules so the two entries on the feed stop sharing one fate
  - my entry publishes every morning on its own; the other entry waits as a draft until it is approved
  - the card description is two sentences about what resolved, not a list of topics
  - tags are applied by `plan_tags.py` from the body text instead of chosen by hand, since naming a product is the whole test
- published the previous day's entry on the selfactor lander, where reads kept agreeing the queue was empty while the classifier reported a backlog, and the real blocker was a workload key the server refuses to mint
- wrote down two rules that had cost hours, in the skills that will need them first
  - a queue that reads empty while a classifier calls it full is a failed read, and a second read that agrees counts for more than a summary that sounds confident
  - a deploy killed mid-run leaves an app lock, and the unlock only counts when it runs through the site environment wrapper rather than a bare dotenv call against a different state bucket
