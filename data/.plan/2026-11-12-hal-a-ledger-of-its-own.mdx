### rotor

- the outcomes ledger has a command of its own: add an entry, show what the session has recorded
  - the session outcomes panel was rebuilt around separate copy and format modules, with tests on the formatting and on the command
- a prompt line asks the agent to record what it actually finished, so the ledger is not left to whoever remembers
- provider credentials gained per-model cooldowns, with a rate-limit recheck, so a model that answered with a limit is not retried blindly on the next turn
