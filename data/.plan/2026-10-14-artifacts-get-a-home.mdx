### selfactor

- **artifacts:** the artifact system has its own host now, the repo view has an artifacts tab, the viewer keeps its media inside the frame, and a session can publish its own evidence through a bundled artifact skill.
- **landing:** the landed-worktree sweep and the cleanup refusal path were rebuilt so a refusal reports its exact cause, and work that is complete but unmerged now queues for admission instead of sitting invisible.
  - still blocked: the lander's own claim is rejected because its workload token is not installed, so the reconcile sweep, the cleanup refusal guard, the attention-patch reader and sgit's lock recovery are finished on branches and not yet on main.
- **cli:** `self chat resume --list` reads the real session index; a stale operator token falls back to the session exchange; a product refresh says why it could not retain publication custody; the launchd path waits as long as launchd does; the splash is productized with an optional cover fit.
- **agents:** one shim profile had carried an old model in its own config for weeks while the desktop covered for it; it is on the current model now.
- **research:** two competitor codebases read against our own factory and written up as a comparison; the brain-index catalog read and the research PRD specified and registered.

### gdock

- the modal got its theme overlay, its fidelity restored, and its own evidence: the mockups and eleven reference screenshots archived inside the project with a manifest.
- the three requested fixes landed: native resume picker, repo-mode sidebar settings, panel colors. along with tag-filter overflow, toolbar sections that wrap at narrow widths, and the org icon out of the repo header.
- the Claude model picker takes its catalog from the installed binary, so the current models show up; a pending gdock build installs itself when gdock quits.

### selfagents

- the fork is rebranded, its PRD registered, and its installation home is being retargeted to one dedicated directory.
- profile-owned animated sky, chatless org instances stop vanishing from the roster, desktop dev survives denied process inspection, icons refreshed.

### sgit

- stale git-lock recovery is scoped to the repository that owns the lock instead of sweeping across all of them.

### rr

- auth: remember-me persistence wired through the first load and its migration; both baseline stacks typecheck again.
