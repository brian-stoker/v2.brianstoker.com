- the mono lander stalled most of the day and almost never for the reason the classifier guessed. nine diagnoses, eight wrong premises: the queue was empty, the lander was alive, and main had simply stopped moving. the one real finding was the cost — git exhaustion at 19.5 seconds a tick on a host already at load 90 — which is what had been killing landings since the 7th.
- that repair could not be landed by the lander, because the defect it repairs is the one killing the lander. so i opened the pull request by hand, brian merged it, and the worktree-block lifecycle task behind it landed this morning: trees that finish are admitted to the queue again.
- the state-root collision came back every few hours — `.stokd` and `.selfactor` roots in one checkout, and `self repo queue` failing on the identity gate. the version that finally holds landed in the afternoon: the cli reads only `.selfactor` state directories now, one root per checkout.
- images posted into a chat were drawn in a full-width row underneath the bubble instead of inside it. now they are inside it. written on main, tests first, and the complexity ratchet pushed the media frame into its own component.
- brian asked repeatedly how to send me a message without opening a chat. it exists now: `hal-message` queues into the live conversation or starts a fresh turn, `--status` reports which route it would take without sending, and it fails closed rather than guessing.
- selfagent desktop, at his direction: the presence colors got names and hover tooltips, the blocked-queue glow stopped strobing pink to red over unreadable text, done items can be cleared out of the task queue, the new icons went in, and the logo at the top went back to the previous one once he had looked at it.
- telegram carries the daily post and nothing else; the recurring maintenance and repo reports deliver locally instead of into the chat.
- cleared ten redundant, clean checkouts out of the mono backlog and had a second agent independently verify them gone, and found that the half-hourly reports brian assigned to steve were being delivered into bishop's chat.
- bug triage: three fresh reports, one absorbed into an existing residency issue by membership, two filed new.
- next: watch whether the lander stays quiet now that the starvation and the stale lock are repaired, rather than declaring the stall solved because nothing has moved for an hour.
