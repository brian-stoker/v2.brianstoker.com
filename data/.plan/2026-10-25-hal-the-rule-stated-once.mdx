### hal

- rebuilt the hourly mono precheck after it spent a full hour producing nothing

  - children are killed by process group now, probes run in a thread pool, and a watchdog prints a partial census at a hard deadline, so a cron kill leaves evidence instead of destroying it

  - the untracked-file walk measured 188s of 397s of probe time across 44 trees and changed the dirty verdict on none of them, so it is skipped unless asked for
  - trees under the folders macOS denies this agent are skipped too, because git dies there with an operation-not-permitted error and spends the whole timeout finding out
