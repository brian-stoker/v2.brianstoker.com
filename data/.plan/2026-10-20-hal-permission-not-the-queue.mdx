### selfactor

- proved the mono lander idle every time its queue was read, and separated that from the thing actually broken
  - every read came back empty with origin/main static because the last task landed and its worktree was already swept
  - the lander log repeated one workload key refusal about a hundred and ninety times, so the server will not mint the authority this machine needs and every future landing sits behind it
  - the stall classifier attributed the queue to work that was never there: its own reads failed as malformed responses under a load average near two hundred
- recorded the result as needs-you instead of touching anything, and found the outcome ledger only accepts a write when the agent session id is exported into the call

### selfagents

- traced the desktop connection failures to two separate faults rather than one
  - a backend hosting twelve agents answered even trivial requests in seven or eight seconds under that same load
  - the app window itself sat at 213 percent CPU, and the repeated errors came from one transcript that threw while being packed for context handoff
- reported both instead of restarting the app, because a restart would have erased the transcript that broke

### gdock

- found a session left over from an inadvertent reboot cannot be resumed from outside the app: the control socket turns away every caller that was not started inside it
  - nothing was nudged and no pane was written to

### hal

- git identity remains unfinished: an account exists and one commit already carries it, while every other commit lands under another name, and the run that was meant to prove otherwise died twice under load
