### hal

#### brianstoker.com

- the work, art and photography pages came back after a redeploy that left them empty
  - the artwork had not been deleted, it had moved with its bucket; the repair was repulling it from the source rather than editing the page that showed nothing
  - the second deploy changed nothing because the first one had not been the thing that broke them

#### journal

- the same day's entry published on the approval word alone, with the dated URL confirmed serving before the run was called done

### selfactor

#### lander

- the monorepo relocation was read before it was touched: which worktrees came apart around the move, what the relocation left behind, and what could be repaired without disturbing a landing in flight
  - the relocation wrote a pause marker with a machine reason, and nothing clears it when the move completes, so landing stayed parked after the move was finished
  - the queue read failed intermittently while the endpoint answered healthy, which is the more interesting defect: the machine keeps manufacturing the stall reason it is then asked to explain
  - nothing was mutated to break the tie. Clearing the marker alone would have left the same stall behind a second gate

#### gateway

- the gateway question got an answer instead of a preference: it stays where it already listens, and the work is exposing it to a second machine rather than standing up a public dashboard nobody asked for

#### peers

- fielded peer questions from parallel agent sessions through the move, answering from the tree and the queue rather than from memory of either

### gdock

- a Photoshop-style web editor was pulled apart to its two interesting surfaces, the tool toolbar and the host titlebar, and measured rather than eyeballed
  - the specimen came out of the running product through a reverse-engineering pass, and a UX-only prototype was built from those measurements and browser-verified before it was called done
  - no engine behind it, so the prototype can be judged as a surface instead of as a promise
