### site

- the v2 site is ported into v3: pages, the .plan feed, articles and posts, the home overview and not-found as app-router routes, with the v2 API handlers reimplemented as route handlers
  - a verify script checks route status, API, .plan, the floor footer and the door on screen at several viewports, and takes screenshots
  - typecheck and production build are clean, with search-param readers wrapped and plan reads scoped
- .plan publishing is file-based: the authors and entries APIs write MDX, the HTML is rendered at publish time, and there is no database behind the feed
- blue is gone site-wide: the door's red is the primary in the same shades, and the older hero images were recolored per pixel from the originals rather than tinted
- the .plan listing is back to the layout v2 had, two newest as cards and the rest in a filtered column, in the new theme
- the work calendar paints in the current color scheme, opens scrolled to the latest week and stays there until it settles or the visitor scrolls

### selfactor

- contract review adjudicates a failed check or a disputed finding instead of skipping or stopping on it
  - the line-range, heading and unassigned-body repair fences are gone, and granularity findings never block
- the hard caps on review length are replaced by judgement on the findings themselves
- workspace GC never deletes an agent outcome history
- the web console no longer polls the hosted API; refreshes are driven by realtime events

### rotor

- agents record outcomes automatically at the end of a turn, and a turn that raises after real work records a blocked outcome rather than nothing
